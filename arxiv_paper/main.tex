\title{Adaptive Fraud Detection Under Delayed Verification: A Reproducible Evaluation Protocol}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Sheikh Joynal Abedin Sumon}}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We describe an evaluation framework for fraud classification under changing distributions
and delayed feedback. Version 0.4 corrects baseline parity, original-decision verification,
and uncertainty estimation. This manuscript is a protocol draft; no new real-data results
or publication-ready conclusions are asserted.
\end{abstract}
\section{Protocol}
All policies share an initial fitted XGBoost estimator. Chronological fitting precedes a
separate calibration and threshold-validation partition. Predictions on future slices
are recorded before labels become available. Only matured labels enter retraining.
Static, periodic, every-slice, sliding-window and drift-triggered strategies are compared.
\section{Experiments}
Evaluate multiple seeds and non-overlapping future periods. Compare recent-memory
windows, age weighting, matched retrain caps, fixed and stochastic delays, and feature
availability ablations. Report average precision, precision, recall, F1, review-budget
performance, calibration and measured training time. Paired seed/time-block bootstrap
intervals summarize observed-stream uncertainty; seeds are not independent populations.
\section{Reproducibility}
Each run stores dataset fingerprints, environment versions, configuration, predictions,
labels, thresholds, detector events and retraining durations. Figures are regenerated
from that run's ledger. Synthetic smoke experiments establish execution only.
\section{Limitations and Results Status}
Real-data experiments remain to be rerun. Historical v0.3 claims are archived and are
not evidence for the corrected protocol. SHAP and PSI are diagnostic rather than causal.
Incomplete identities, temporal dependence, selective labels and operational capacity
need further empirical investigation. No production latency SLA is asserted.
